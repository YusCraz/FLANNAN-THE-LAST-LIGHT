# FLANNAN: THE LAST LIGHT

> An AI-directed interactive horror mystery inspired by the real Flannan Isles lighthouse disappearance.

**Status:** 🚧 In Development  
**Built for:** Rive Halloween Challenge 2026

---

## Overview

**FLANNAN: THE LAST LIGHT** is a browser-based cinematic horror experience where an AI acts as the main story director.

Instead of freely controlling a character, the player experiences the story through first-person POV scenes, animations, sound, narration, and controlled choices.

The AI manages the experience by deciding:

- what happens next
- which scene should play
- how the story is narrated
- when tension should increase
- when sound or visual effects should trigger
- which clues should appear
- how the story reacts to previous player choices

The goal is to combine **AI Engineering, interactive storytelling, real historical mystery, and Rive animation** into one adaptive horror experience.

---

## Core Idea

The player enters the mystery surrounding the disappearance of the three lighthouse keepers of the Flannan Isles.

The AI acts as the player's guide.

At first, it appears helpful.

As the story progresses, the player may begin questioning whether the AI itself can be trusted.
